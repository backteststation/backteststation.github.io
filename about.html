About us

Backtest Station exists because we kept seeing the same thing: beautiful backtests, real money, and results that looked nothing alike.

Built by a trader, for traders

Backtest Station was founded and is run by a trader with 13 years in the markets: Forex, crypto mining and trading, listed and over-the-counter stocks, and private company shares.

Along the way came years of designing, coding and running trading bots, several of them live today on crypto and stock markets. That is where the lessons in our reports come from: a strategy that looks perfect on paper, fees that quietly eat the edge, a signal that only works because it saw the next candle.

We started Backtest Station to give every trader the second opinion we always wanted before trusting a bot with real money.

What we stand for

Independent

We do not sell bots, signals or courses, and we never take money from a bot seller for a verdict. Our only product is the test.

Honest about limits

Every report shows a confidence level, an optimistic and a pessimistic case, and the reasons a result could differ. We test the past, not the future.

Verifiable

Every report carries a QR code and a check link, so nobody can quietly edit our numbers. Our methodology is public.

Your files stay yours

Code and trade files are never published. We never ask for exchange keys or passwords, and unknown code only runs in an isolated environment with no internet access. See privacy.
