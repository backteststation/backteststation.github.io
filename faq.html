How can someone check that a report is real?

Every report has a QR code and a report ID with a code. Scan it, or enter both on our verify page, to see the verdict and numbers we issued.

What do PASS, CAUTION and FAIL mean?

PASS: the edge held up in this test. CAUTION: mixed results or too little evidence. FAIL: no edge, or data that cannot be trusted, such as signals that use future candles or prices that never traded.

What is the test confidence percentage?

How sure we are that the numbers are what you would really have had in that period. A trade list checked to the second is close to 100%; a strategy replayed on candles is a little lower. The report lists every reason. Below 85% we mark the result as indicative only.

What can I send?

A trade history (CSV or Excel export from an exchange or a signal channel), a Freqtrade strategy (.py), a Pine Script, or a Forex / gold / oil trade history (a MetaTrader 5 export works as it is). Backtests of MT4 / MT5 Expert Advisor files are coming soon.

Will my code be published?

Never. When you order, you choose whether the result may be public (with your bot's name), anonymous, or private. Only the result can be shown, not the code.

Do you run my code with my API keys?

No. We never ask for exchange keys. Code is read first, and if it needs to run, it runs in an isolated environment without internet access or keys.

How long does it take?

Trade-history checks are automatic and usually ready in minutes. Strategy backtests take from a few hours to two days, depending on the period. See turnaround.

Why do most showcase bots lose money?

They are free example strategies, run with their default settings. Many were written to show how a feature works, not to trade real money. That is exactly why testing matters, and why your own tuned or paid bot deserves its own test.

Can a good backtest guarantee profit?

No. A backtest shows how rules would have behaved in the past. Markets change. We report the past honestly; we do not predict the future.
