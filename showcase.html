How to read a card

Edge Score (0–100)

Combines profit after fees, consistency across years, drawdown, number of trades and whether the strategy beat simply holding the same coins. Full method.

All tests: 1,000 USDT, up to 5 trades at once, 0.1% fee per side, Binance spot data. Results describe one set of settings over one period. Other settings, coins or periods can give different results. Nothing here is a recommendation to use or avoid any strategy.

For strategy authors

We test free, public strategies with the settings they ship with. If you wrote one of these and think other settings, coins or timeframes represent it better, send them to @BacktestStationBot. We will retest it free and update the report. You can also ask us to remove a result.

Removal needs proof of ownership. So that nobody can take down a result they do not own, we ask the author to show they control the strategy: for example, a short note or commit with a code we give you in the strategy's own repository, a message from the account that publishes it, or, for a paid bot, from the official sales page or channel. Retest requests with better settings are open to anyone.

Paid or commercial bots are only shown here with the owner's consent, or when the customer who ordered the test chose to make it public. Otherwise they stay private or anonymous.
