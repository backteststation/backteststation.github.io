What we check

Strategy backtest

Send your Freqtrade or TradingView Pine Script strategy. It runs automatically on years of data across 20+ coins or Forex, with real fees and a fee stress test.

Track-record check

Got a list of trades someone claims to have made? We match every entry and exit against real prices, down to the second, and flag prices that never happened.

Future-data check

Many strategies look great only because they peek at candles they could not have seen live. We run a lookahead test and say so when they do.

Your coins, to the second

Test on our 20 main coins, or name the coins you trade. Want more detail? We can replay your strategy on 1-second candles. Custom coins and 1-second runs take longer.

Three outcomes, not one

Real trading is never as clean as a backtest. Each report shows an optimistic, a normal and a pessimistic result, with what moves them: fills, slippage, fees and the market.

Forex, gold and oil too

Selling or buying a MetaTrader EA or a Forex signal? Send its trade history (a MetaTrader 5 export works as it is) and we check every entry and exit against real bid and ask prices of that minute, for Forex pairs, gold, silver, oil and the main indices.

A report nobody can fake

Every report carries a QR code and a check link. Anyone you show it to can confirm on our site that the verdict and numbers are the ones we issued.
