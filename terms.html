4. Files you send

We test only the kinds of files listed in our bot and on the guides page, sent as follows:

  • Trade histories: the export of the exchange, broker or bot (CSV, Excel, HTML statement or JSON), or a PDF or a clear picture of the trade table with its header row.
  • Strategies: one Freqtrade strategy file (.py), one TradingView Pine Script strategy, or one MetaTrader 5 Expert Advisor (.mq5 as a single file with its own includes merged in, or the compiled .ex5).
  • Zip files only to make a file smaller, holding that one file: not a whole project, repository or program (at most 200 files and 200 MB unpacked).
  • Size: up to 20 MB per file, which is Telegram's limit for files sent to bots.
  • No exchange API keys, passwords, or other people's personal data.

Which prices we test with. Backtests and checks use Binance prices for crypto (spot for strategies, USDT-M futures for trade histories) and Dukascopy bid/ask prices for Forex, metals, oil and indices. Some bots depend on the data of particular exchanges and cannot be tested on one price history: arbitrage between exchanges, market making, bots that need order books, funding or prices of several exchanges at the same moment, and futures strategies built around one exchange's own contract. These are not backtested; their real trade history from each exchange can be checked. Results on another exchange or broker can differ, and we allow for the usual difference.

We do not install or run other programs: whole bot projects, arbitrage bots and bots of other platforms are checked through their trade history, not run. A file that does not follow these rules may not be tested, or may produce a report that does not represent the strategy, and we are not responsible for that. If we cannot test a file at all, the test is not counted: a free test stays free, and a paid test stays on your account.
